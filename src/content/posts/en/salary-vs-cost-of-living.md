The first money surprise: salaries are a lot higher than in France for the same position.

The second surprise: so is the cost of living. Rent especially is higher than what you would pay in most of France.

But when I put it all together, at an equal job I live better here in Florida than I would in a big French city. The gap between what you earn and what you spend lands in your favor.

A few things to keep in mind when you compare offers:

* Your health insurance premium usually comes out of your paycheck (see [the health insurance article](../health-insurance-explained/)).
* Rent listings often leave out a lot of monthly fees (see [the housing article](../finding-a-place-to-live/)).
* Tipping adds up if you eat out a lot (see [the tipping article](../tipping-in-the-us/)).
* I can only speak for Florida. Big coastal cities like New York or San Francisco are a different equation.
