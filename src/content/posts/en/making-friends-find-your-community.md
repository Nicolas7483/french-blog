The thing I noticed after two years here: American social life runs on communities.

The country is individualistic overall. But people gather in smaller groups built around one shared interest, and that is where they bond. It can be climbing, the gym, hiking, running, Dungeons and Dragons, Magic: The Gathering, video games, anything. That community is how you build a circle.

You see the same pattern later in life with neighborhoods, which often work like their own small separate communities.

This is a real difference with France, where a lot of friendships come from school, from where you grew up, or from friends of friends you have known for years. When you move to the US at 23 or 25, you have none of that. So you have to go find a community.

## What I tried first

In my first year I joined a touch rugby group and a chess club, and I used the Meetup app to find groups and meet new people. It worked pretty well for meeting people.

The catch: you meet people of all ages. In those groups I was often with people in their 30s and 40s who had families. That is great for the activity itself. It is less great for hanging out afterward, because their lives run on a different schedule.

## What actually worked

My real friend group came through one person. My partner, who is American, introduced me to a friend of hers. That friend's roommate had a boyfriend, the four of us started hanging out more and more, and we started going to kava bars together (kava is a nonalcoholic root drink from the Pacific Islands, and kava bars are a big social thing in Florida).

From there it snowballed. Whenever I met someone new, I invited them along. They brought people. That is how the circle grew.

## What I would tell you

* Join activity groups to get out and meet people, but do not expect your core friends to come from there.
* Look for one connector: one person who can introduce you to their people.
* Be the one who invites. Americans are friendly and will usually say yes, but someone has to organize.
* Pick activities with people your age when you can, if you want friends you will also hang out with outside the activity.
