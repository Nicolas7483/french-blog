I am a French guy in my twenties. I grew up and studied in France, went to business school, and moved to Florida a couple of years ago for a job.

Living in the US was a dream of mine, and I love it here. That does not mean everything is better. Some things are genuinely better, some are worse, and most are just different in ways that trip you up if nobody warns you.

I am not a lawyer, a recruiter, a doctor or a financial advisor. Everything here is my own experience and my own opinion. Where something has legal or money consequences (visas especially), I point you to the official source. Use my notes to know what questions to ask, not as the final answer.

If one or two people land here and have an easier first year because of it, the site did its job.
